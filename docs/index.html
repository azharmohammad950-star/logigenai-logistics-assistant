06 / SCOPE & SAFEGUARDS

Useful assistance. No invented certainty.

LogiGenAI is an academic decision-support prototype. Its demonstration outputs are simulated and must not be treated as verified operational advice.

01

Use supplied facts

Outputs are grounded in scenario details entered by the user. Missing information should be checked, not assumed.

02

Verify documents and rules

The documentation list is an operational prompt, not a definitive legal, customs or regulatory checklist.

03

Confirm before communicating

Check live shipment milestones, carrier availability, contractual commitments and customer-specific requirements before action.
